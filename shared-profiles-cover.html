Quickstart

Three steps. Then nobody asks twice.

1

Drop the folder in

Unzip shared-profiles/ into ~/.claude/skills/, or upload the zip on claude.ai under Settings → Capabilities → Skills.

2

Say who you are

Set up my shared profiles. My business is Acme Plumbing & Heating.

Claude creates ~/.indieops/, fills in what it already knows, and asks only for what's missing.

3

Give it a look

Give Acme a brand theme. Warm, trustworthy, not corporate.

Pick a starting design from a shortlist, add your colour and logo, and open the live preview.

No secrets, ever. Passwords, API keys and card details never go in this folder. It's plain text in your home folder, and it will end up in a backup or a screen share one day.
What you get

Who you are, what they do, how it looks.

A page per business

Real hours, domains, DNS, services and the voice the business uses. Yours or a client's, one folder each.

A brand theme

A colour for every job, open fonts, type sizes, corners, spacing and your logo, with dark mode worked out and readable.

A live preview

Your brand on a spec sheet, social posts, a landing page, a report and an email. Change anything, copy the edits back.

Brand themes

Start from a real design language.

Every theme begins with a design from awesome-design-md, VoltAgent's open collection of DESIGN.md files: the colours, type, spacing and rules of well-known products, free under the MIT licence. You pick the one that feels right; your colour, fonts and logo sit on top as your own layer, so you can switch designs later and keep them. Nothing you make ever wears the starting design's name or logo.
